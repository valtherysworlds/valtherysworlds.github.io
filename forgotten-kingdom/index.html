The World

No one remembers the kingdom's name. Only that centuries ago, a king ruled from that castle on the cliff — and that one night, without war or siege, his entire people vanished.

The lights in the windows still burn. The banners hang still in the towers. The fog over the valley never fully lifts, as if the kingdom itself refuses to show what happened that night.

Those who draw near say they feel watched from the towers — but never see anyone.

The Forgotten Kingdom appears on no map. It only lets itself be found by those who have already stopped looking.

An original soundtrack in five acts, with a living scene for each part of the journey. No vocals, no narration.
