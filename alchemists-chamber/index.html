The World

Behind an unmarked wall, in a city whose map keeps changing, there is a chamber that should not exist.

The alchemist who built it vanished mid-experiment, and nothing inside has stopped since. The alembic never finishes distilling. A notebook on the workbench rewrites its own formula every night. A locked drawer hums quietly, as if something inside were still breathing. And the flame beneath the cauldron shifts color on its own, following a rhythm no one set.

The same silver-blue glow fills every corner of the chamber — the same glow, some say, that lingers over a distant castle under a different moon.

An original soundtrack in five acts, with a living scene for each part of the journey. No vocals, no narration.
