The World

There is a library that cannot be entered from outside. No door opens onto it, no road leads to it — and yet its candles never go out.

Its keeper, a wizard no book remembers by name, has been gone longer than anyone can say. The shelves still wait for him. Somewhere beyond the iron gate, a chained book turns its own pages.

Deep inside, beneath a dome of slow-turning constellations, lies a map of a kingdom with no name — a castle on a cliff, a valley where the mist never lifts. Whoever drew it knew a place the world has forgotten.

An original soundtrack in five acts, with a living scene for each part of the journey. No vocals, no narration.
