The World

In the middle of a black lake stands a castle that can only be reached while the moon is in the sky. When the moon rises, a stone path appears across the water. Before dawn, it sinks again.

The windows glow, yet there are no candles. Every light in the castle is moonlight that came in and never left. In the courtyard, silver flowers open only at night, tended by hands no one has ever seen. Once each night, the bells of the tower ring, though their ropes rotted away centuries ago.

Those who leave before dawn say it felt like waking from a dream they would give anything to return to. No one speaks of those who stayed.

It is not a place to fear. It feels like entering someone else's dream, where the castle sleeps and you are the only one awake.

From the highest tower, on a clear night, a faraway castle can be seen on a cliff, its lights still burning.

An original soundtrack in five acts, with a living scene for each part of the journey. No vocals, no narration.
